Subscriptions & Cancellation

How subscriptions work

Wingman offers three subscription tiers — Free, Plus (£2.99/month), and Pro (£6.99/month). Paid subscriptions are billed monthly through the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android) and renew automatically until cancelled.

TierPriceBillingKey features
Free£0—Basic vault, PIN/biometric lock, up to 50 items
Plus£2.99/moMonthly, auto-renewsUnlimited vault, AI scanning, auto-categorisation
Pro£6.99/moMonthly, auto-renewsEverything in Plus + Intruder Snapshot, Duress PIN, cloud backup

How to cancel

You can cancel your subscription at any time through the platform you subscribed on:

  • iOS (App Store): Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Wingman → Cancel
  • Android (Google Play): Play Store → Account → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Wingman → Cancel

Your access continues until the end of your current billing period. We do not offer partial-period refunds on cancellation — access simply stops at the next renewal date.

Free trial (if offered)

When a free trial is offered, your subscription begins automatically at the end of the trial period unless you cancel before it expires. Cancel any time before the trial ends to avoid being charged.

Refunds

All subscription payments are processed by Apple or Google, not by Wingman directly. This means refund requests must be submitted to the relevant platform, not to us. We cannot issue refunds from our side.

How to request a refund

  • iOS / App Store: Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, find the Wingman charge, and select "Request a refund".
  • Android / Google Play: Visit Google Play refund page or open the Play Store → Order history → Request refund.

When we support your refund request

Although the platforms make the final refund decision, we will actively support your request in the following circumstances:

  • You were charged for a renewal after cancelling and the cancellation was confirmed before the billing date.
  • You experienced a technical fault that prevented you from accessing premium features for a significant portion of your billing period, and you reported it to us.
  • You were charged for a free trial you believe you cancelled in time.
  • The charge was unauthorised or fraudulent.
  • You are within 14 days of your first subscription charge and have not made substantial use of premium features (in line with your statutory rights under the UK Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013).

Your statutory rights (UK & EU)

If you are a consumer in the UK or EU, you have a 14-day right of withdrawal from a digital service purchase, unless you expressly consented to the service starting immediately and acknowledged that this waives your withdrawal right. By subscribing and starting to use Wingman Plus or Pro features immediately, you agree that the service begins immediately and acknowledge this may affect your right of withdrawal.

Nothing in this policy limits your statutory rights. If you believe your statutory rights entitle you to a refund, contact us and we will assist.

What we cannot refund

  • Charges from prior billing periods where premium features were available and functional.
  • Charges arising from a failure to cancel before auto-renewal (though we encourage you to contact us if you feel this was unfair).
  • Charges on accounts that have used Pro features extensively during the billing period.

Returns Policy

Wingman is a digital app — there is no physical product to return. There are no delivery charges, shipping costs, or physical goods associated with any Wingman subscription or purchase.

If you purchased Wingman through the App Store or Google Play and no longer wish to use it, you may:

  • Cancel your subscription as described in Section 1 above.
  • Delete the app from your device at any time. Deleting the app removes all locally stored vault contents permanently.
  • Request a refund via your platform as described in Section 2 above.

Note on vault data: Deleting the app is irreversible if you do not have cloud backup enabled (Pro tier). We strongly recommend exporting or backing up vault contents before deleting the app.

Warranty & Service Guarantee

What we warrant

We warrant that Wingman will perform materially in accordance with its published feature description during any paid subscription period. Specifically:

  • The vault will encrypt and store content using AES-256-GCM encryption as described.
  • PIN and biometric authentication will function on supported devices and OS versions.
  • Features included in your subscription tier (Plus or Pro) will be accessible while your subscription is active.

Service uptime

Wingman operates primarily on-device and does not depend on our servers for core vault functionality. Local encryption, locking, and vault access do not require an internet connection. Cloud backup (Pro tier) and RevenueCat subscription verification require internet access.

We aim for 99.9% availability of cloud-dependent features but do not provide a formal SLA for consumer subscriptions.

Supported platforms

  • iOS: iOS 15 and above on iPhone and iPad.
  • Android: Android 10 (API level 29) and above.

We cannot warrant functionality on jailbroken or rooted devices, as these may bypass the security protections that Wingman relies on.

Bug fixes & updates

We are committed to fixing material defects that affect core functionality (vault access, encryption, authentication) promptly. Minor bugs and cosmetic issues will be addressed in scheduled updates. We do not charge separately for app updates.

Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, our liability for any claim arising from your use of Wingman is limited to the subscription fees you paid in the 3 months preceding the claim.

We are not liable for:

  • Loss of vault contents due to device loss, damage, or failure where no cloud backup was enabled.
  • Loss of access to the vault due to forgotten PIN where no recovery method was configured.
  • Indirect or consequential losses.

Nothing in this section limits liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, fraud, or any other liability that cannot be excluded by law.

Consumer rights

UK consumers have rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 regarding digital content. If Wingman does not conform to its description, you may be entitled to a repair, replacement, or refund. Contact us at support@wingman.app and we will work to resolve the issue.
