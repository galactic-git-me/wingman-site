QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Wingman, explained.

Everything you need to know about creating a vault, scanning your gallery, and choosing the right plan.

Do I need an account to use Wingman?

Yes. Free and Pro users create an account in the app. Google sign-in is available to make setup quick and to help associate your vault with your account.

Why do I initially see 60 photos?

Wingman uses batches so a large gallery remains responsive and easy to review. It is not a 60-photo library limit: use “Load next 60 photos” to continue through the rest of your library.

Does Wingman delete my originals immediately?

No. You choose the photos, Wingman copies them first, and the app asks you to confirm before originals are moved or removed. You stay in control of that decision.

What do Free and Pro include?

Free includes one scan per month and up to 50 items in the vault. Pro is for users who need higher limits and the complete premium feature set. The app shows the available options before purchase.

Does Wingman upload my photos?

Wingman is designed around a private vault on your device. Review the in-app permission prompts and the Privacy Policy for the precise data handling details that apply to your build.

How are prices shown in other countries?

Purchases are handled by Google Play. The final local price, including any applicable taxes, is shown by Google Play at checkout. Promotional prices may vary by country.

How do I manage or cancel Pro?

Open Google Play, go to your profile, choose Payments & subscriptions, then Subscriptions. Select Wingman to manage or cancel the subscription.

Where can I get help?

Visit our Support page and include your device model, app version, and a description of what happened. Please do not send private photos or vault contents.
