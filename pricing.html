Good to know

Questions, answered.

Can I use Wingman for free?

Yes. The Free plan includes one gallery scan each month, up to 50 vault items, and the private encrypted vault with PIN and decoy vault protection.

What does Pro unlock?

Pro gives you unlimited scans and vault capacity, plus Partner Mode, Auto-scan, video scanning, Intruder Snap, and Fake App Mode.

Is Pro a subscription?

Choose £4 monthly or £40 annually. Both are auto-renewing subscriptions managed through Google Play. You can cancel in your Google Play subscriptions.

Is there a lifetime option?

Yes. Wingman Pro Lifetime is available as a one-time £100 purchase, with no recurring payments.
