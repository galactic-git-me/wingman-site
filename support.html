Frequently asked questions

I forgot my PIN — can I recover my vault?
If you do not have cloud backup enabled (Pro tier) and no recovery key saved, vault contents cannot be recovered. This is by design — your PIN is the only key. If you have cloud backup enabled, you can restore from your encrypted backup after resetting the app.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed through the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android), not through the app itself. On iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Wingman → Cancel. On Android: Play Store → Account → Payments & subscriptions → Wingman → Cancel.
Can I request a refund?
Refunds are processed by Apple or Google. On iOS, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, use the Google Play refund request flow. See our Returns & Refunds page for full details on when we support refund requests.
Will Wingman share my photos with anyone?
No. All vault contents are encrypted on your device and never transmitted to our servers. We cannot see your photos. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
What happens to my data if I delete the app?
All locally stored vault contents are permanently deleted when you remove the app. If you have Pro cloud backup enabled, your encrypted backup will remain in cloud storage until you explicitly delete it. We recommend exporting important content before deleting the app.
My subscription is active but I can't access Pro features.
Try: (1) Force-close and reopen the app. (2) Go to Settings → Restore Purchases inside the app. (3) Ensure you're signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account used to subscribe. If the problem persists, email us at support@wingman.app and include your order ID from the App Store or Play Store receipt.
Is Wingman available on iPad or tablet?
Wingman is available on iOS (iPhone and iPad, iOS 15+) and Android (10+). The same subscription covers all your devices signed in with the same account.
